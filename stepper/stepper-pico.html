Stepper motor

Do Pico Output Movement

What does it do?

A stepper motor turns in small, repeatable steps instead of spinning freely.

Energising its coils in sequence moves the shaft one step at a time and holds its position.

The ULN2003 board supplies the coil current for the common 28BYJ-48 motor shown here.

Code

Raspberry Pi Pico

from picozero import Stepper

motor = Stepper((15, 14, 13, 12))

motor.step(100)
Call motor.step(100) again to keep moving. Add direction="ccw" to turn the other way.

Wiring

Pico
IN1-IN4 → GP 15, 14, 13, 12 (pins 20, 19, 17, 16); driver → VBUS (pin 40, bottom + rail) and GND (pin 18)
