Active buzzer

Do Pico blocks Sound

What does it do?

An active buzzer makes one built-in tone. Switch it on and off for warning beeps or rhythms; its pitch stays the same.

Recognise it: check the packet for active or built-in oscillator. Shape, stickers and + marks do not prove the type.

Use a labelled 3.3 V GPIO-compatible buzzer.
